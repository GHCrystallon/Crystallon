## Inputs

- Voxels (Voxels) - [twisted box] - Voxels to be filled with unit cells
- Geometry (Geometry) - [mesh] - Voxelized geometry used to find intersections

## Outputs

- Boundary Voxels (Boundary Voxels) - [generic data] - Voxels near the geometry boundary
